Monthly Care Plan

Your website is live — we'll help you keep it current

Publishing a website is only the start. Menu changes, new prices, seasonal notices, fresh photos — small, regular updates are what keep a site feeling like your business today.

If you don't have time to make those updates yourself, our Monthly Care Plan takes care of them for you. It is our single monthly plan — the one monthly fee covers hosting, updates and fixes, plus a monthly Google review check and one notice post per month. There is no second monthly charge on top of it.

Price

$49/month (USD)

  • No minimum term. Cancel anytime.
  • Paid by credit card through Stripe's secure checkout, billed automatically each month.
  • This is separate from the website build fee ($250 to start, $250 after your approval). To order a website, please use the usual page: https://brightfrontdesign.com/start/

What's included

  • Hosting & upkeepYour site's hosting, plus small fixes such as layout glitches, are included.
  • Site updatesSend us changes to opening hours, holidays, or announcements by email and we'll apply them for you.
  • Menu & price changesNew items, revised prices, and seasonal menu swaps.
  • Photo swapsSend us your new photos and we'll publish or replace them to match the look of your site.
  • Monthly Google review checkOnce a month we check your new Google reviews and report them to you by email. We can also help draft a reply if you'd like.
  • One notice post per monthOne seasonal notice or event post per month on your site's news/blog section. You send the photos and key points; we prepare the text.

Work beyond everyday updates — such as adding large new sections or a redesign — is quoted separately in advance. We never charge for it without your agreement.

How it works

  1. Subscribe using the button below (you'll be taken to Stripe's secure checkout).
  2. Once your first payment is confirmed, we'll email you with the details.
  3. From then on, just email us what you'd like changed. We aim to apply updates within 3 business days and will let you know if something needs a little longer.

FAQ

How do I pay?

By credit card via Stripe, billed automatically each month. Stripe emails your receipts. This is separate from the PayPal payment used for the website build fee.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There is no cancellation fee. Email us to cancel; your plan stays active until the end of the period you've already paid for, and no further charges are made.

What happens to my website if I cancel?

Your website stays online — cancelling only ends the update service and monthly checks. We'll explain any hosting arrangements individually when you cancel.

Are there any other monthly fees on top of this?

No. The care plan ($49/month) is the single monthly fee. It includes hosting, updates and fixes, plus the monthly Google review check and one notice post per month. Only work beyond that scope — such as large new sections — is quoted separately in advance.

How fast are updates applied?

We aim for within 3 business days of your request. Updates that involve photo preparation or writing may take slightly longer; we'll tell you in advance if so.

What's not covered?

Everyday updates are covered. Large additions or a full redesign are quoted separately, only if you ask for them.
