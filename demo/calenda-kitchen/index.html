Story

A family truck, painted like a folk-art parade.

Calenda Kitchen is family-run. Ana makes the salsas. Her sons Leo and Sam run the truck with her. The truck itself is painted with colorful Oaxacan folk-art creatures.

They park at 123 Main St in Downtown, near the the library. Weekday mornings and lunch only — closed Saturday and Sunday.

People come back for the smoky charcoal-grilled flavor, the salsas, the huge burritos, and a friendly crew at the window.
