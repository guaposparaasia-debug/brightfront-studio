Restaurants, cafes & bakeries

An affordable website for your restaurant, cafe or bakery

Hungry people search on their phones and decide in seconds. They want your hours, your menu, a few good photos and a way to call or find you. We build that for you, host it and keep it up to date.

What goes on a restaurant website

  • Hours that are right. Including holiday hours and the day you close early. Email us a change and we update it.
  • Your menu, readable on a phone. Typed out as real text, not a blurry photo of a paper menu, so it is easy to read and search engines can see it.
  • Photos of your food and your room. Phone photos are fine. We crop and size them so the page stays fast.
  • Tap to call and tap for directions. One tap from the page to your phone number or to a map.
  • How to order. Links to the ordering or delivery services you already use, if you use any.

Built for a busy owner

You don't need to learn a website builder or log in to anything. You tell us about the place, send what you have, and we do the layout and the writing. When the specials change, the hours shift for summer or you add a new dish, you email us and we make the change. That's what the $39 a month is for.

Bakeries and cafes often change their offer by the season. A small website that someone else keeps current tends to stay accurate, which matters more than a fancy design.

Your Google listing and social accounts are worth keeping. A website adds one place you control, with your full menu and correct hours, that anyone can open without an app or an account. We wrote more about this in Do I need a website if I already have Instagram?
