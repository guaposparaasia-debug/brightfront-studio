Salons & barbershops

A website for your salon or barbershop

New clients want to see your work, know what a cut or color costs and book without phoning. A simple website with your services, your photos and your booking link does that, and it looks right on the phone in their hand.

What a salon website needs

  • Services and prices. A clear list, so people know what to expect before they sit down.
  • Your work. A gallery of real cuts, colors and styles. Your own phone photos work well.
  • Booking. A button to the booking app you already use, or a tap-to-call button if you take appointments by phone.
  • Hours, address and parking notes. The practical details that save a phone call.
  • Your team. Optional short intros, if clients book a specific stylist or barber.

Is Instagram enough for a salon?

Instagram is great for showing your work, and many clients find salons there. A website complements it: it shows up in Google searches for your area, it works for people who don't use Instagram, and it keeps prices and booking in one place. See Do I need a website if I already have Instagram?
