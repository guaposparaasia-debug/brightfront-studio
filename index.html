FAQ

Questions owners actually ask.

Is the preview really free?

Yes. The homepage preview is free. No card needed, and no obligation. If you don’t love it, you walk away and owe nothing.

How do I pay?

Only after you approve the preview. You pay securely via PayPal. For a one-time website payment, PayPal checkout accepts major cards, and in most countries you do not need a PayPal account. Monthly care works a little differently — see below.

How does monthly care billing work?

Choose a care plan on your package card and subscribe with PayPal. Today you pay only the website price. Care is free for the first month while we finish and launch your site (the first three months with Premium), then PayPal bills the monthly fee automatically — $19, $39, or $69 — on the same date each month. Edits are done within 48 hours, and unused edits don’t roll over.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime, with no contract or cancellation fee. Cancel in your PayPal account under automatic payments, or email [email protected] and we’ll cancel it for you. No further charges are taken after you cancel. Your site and your domain stay yours either way.

Do I need a PayPal account?

For a one-time website payment, usually not: PayPal guest checkout accepts major debit and credit cards in most countries. Monthly care is different because PayPal treats it as an automatic payment. Depending on your country, PayPal may offer a “Debit or Credit Card” option without an account, but in many countries it will ask you to log in or create a free PayPal account (you can still pay with your card inside it). Prefer not to? Pay for the website one-time and email us about care.

How long does a site take?

A Starter page is often ready in about a week once we have your text and photos. Business and Premium take longer if menus, booking tools, or extra pages are involved. We confirm a date in the quote.

Do I have to write everything?

You know the business; we shape it into page copy. Premium includes more writing help. You still approve anything that describes prices, hours, or medical-style services.

Can you add booking or a menu?

Yes. We design the menu on the page and link a booking button to the system you already use, such as a calendar or reservation tool. We do not invent a new booking product.

What does “from $149.50” mean?

During the launch sale (until Oct 31, 2026, US Eastern) every package is 50% off: Starter $149.50 (normally $299), Business $349.50 (normally $699), Premium $649.50 (normally $1,299). Monthly care prices are unchanged. It is the starting price for the listed scope. Extra pages, custom photography, or a complicated booking setup are quoted before work starts.

Do you host the site?

Yes, with any monthly care plan (from $19/mo), which includes hosting, SSL, and an uptime check. Premium includes the first three months of care. Without a care plan, we can launch on a host you already pay for.

Can you get me a domain name?

Yes. Domain setup is $49 plus the domain cost itself (usually $10–15/year; we recommend prepaying 2–3 years). We register the domain, connect it to your site, and can transfer it to your own account anytime — it’s always yours. The exact domain cost is quoted by email before anything is charged. Already have a domain, or prefer to buy it yourself? We connect it for free.

Will it work on phones?

Yes. Every layout is designed phone-first, then expanded for larger screens. That is how most local customers arrive.
