What's included

Exactly what you get, and what you don't.

No fine print. Here is what the $250 + $250 build covers, what you receive, and the things that are separate so nothing surprises you later.

Included in the build

  • A website designed around your shop: your photos, hours, menu or services, location and contact details
  • A layout made for phones first, and checked on small screens before you see it
  • Writing: you send notes, we turn them into clear, friendly text
  • Your photos cropped and sized so the page loads quickly
  • Tap-to-call, a directions link, and links to your Instagram, booking or ordering pages
  • Changes before approval: tell us what isn't right and we fix it before you pay the second $250

What you receive

  • A link to review the finished site, emailed from info@brightfrontdesign.com
  • The live website, hosted by us and served over HTTPS
  • Basic search set-up: a clear page title and description and real, readable text
  • One email address for every later change: info@brightfrontdesign.com

Not included in the price

  • Your domain name's yearly registration fee, if you want a new domain
  • Fees for third-party services you choose, such as booking apps, online ordering or delivery platforms (we link to them)
  • An online shop or taking payments on your website
  • Professional photo shoots or logo design
  • Paid advertising or running your social media accounts

Need one of the things in the last column? Ask before you start, and we'll tell you honestly what's possible.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

What does it cost? +

$250 upfront so we start building, $250 after the website is built and you approve it, then $39 a month for maintenance (hosting, updates and changes). You pay the first $250 on this page after you send your request.

When do I pay what? +

First $250 on this page, right after you send your request, so we can begin. Second $250 only after you've seen the finished website and approved it. The $39/month maintenance starts once the site is approved and live. We email the finished site from info@brightfrontdesign.com.

What does the $39 a month cover? +

Hosting, keeping your site secure and up to date, and changes you request, like new hours, a new menu or fresh photos. It's month to month with no contract, and you can cancel anytime. More detail: what the $39/month covers.

Do I need to supply photos or text? +

Phone photos are enough to start. Tell us what you do, send what you have, and we'll shape it into clear, good-looking pages. You can send more or replace anything later. See how to get a website without doing it yourself.

Will it work on phones? +

Yes. Every website we build is mobile-friendly and tested on small screens first, because that's where most local customers will find you.

Who is this for? +

Small, independent businesses such as cafes, salons, bakeries, food trucks and local stores, and anyone who wants a simple, professional website without doing the work themselves.

How long does it take? +

Once we have your photos and details, we aim to email you the first version within about 3 business days. After that it depends on how quickly you review it and send changes.

Can I ask for changes before I approve? +

Yes. That's the point of approval. Look at the finished site on your phone and tell us what you'd like different. You pay the second $250 only once you're happy with it.

Who writes the text? +

We do. Send us notes in your own words, even a few lines about what you sell, what makes you different and who you serve, and we turn them into clear, friendly text for the site.

I don't have a logo. Is that a problem? +

Not at all. Many small shops don't. We set your shop's name in clean type that matches the site, and you can send a logo later if you get one.

Do I need my own domain name? +

If you already have one, tell us in your request. If you'd like a new one, ask us how to set it up. The domain's yearly registration fee is separate from our prices.

Can my website take bookings or online orders? +

We can link your site to the booking or ordering service you already use, so customers can tap straight through. Those services have their own fees. Taking payments directly on your website isn't part of this offer.

How do I pay? +

Through PayPal. Cards are accepted at PayPal checkout. The first $250 is paid on this page after you send your request; we send the request for the second $250 with your finished site.

How do I ask for an update after launch? +

Email info@brightfrontdesign.com with the change and any new photos or files. Changes are covered by your $39/month. More detail: what the $39/month covers.

Is there a contract? +

No. Maintenance is month to month, and you can cancel anytime.

Will a website get me to the top of Google? +

No one can honestly promise rankings. A website with your real hours, services and location written as text gives search engines more to work with, and it pairs well with a complete Google Business Profile. We set clear titles and descriptions; we don't sell ranking guarantees.

Do I still need a website if I'm on Instagram? +

Instagram is great for showing new work. A website gives everyone, with or without an app, one reliable place for your hours, prices and location. We wrote more in Do I need a website if I already have Instagram?
