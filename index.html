The problem

Customers Google you first.

Before they cross the street, they search your name, your neighbourhood, or “near me.” A missing site, a broken phone page, or a menu that lives only in a photo can send them to the next result. The website is the door they meet before the real one.

They search the nameA clean page with hours, address text, and what you actually sell beats a blank result.
They judge in secondsType, photos, and a clear next step — call, book, or visit — do the talking.
They pick what looks openA current menu or booking link makes a Tuesday afternoon feel looked-after.

FAQ

Questions owners actually ask.

Is the preview really free?

Yes. The homepage preview is free. No card needed, and no obligation. If you don’t love it, you walk away and owe nothing.

How do I pay?

Only after you approve the preview. You pay securely via PayPal. PayPal checkout accepts major cards, and in most countries you do not need a PayPal account.

How long does a site take?

A Starter page is often ready in about a week once we have your text and photos. Business and Premium take longer if menus, booking tools, or extra pages are involved. We confirm a date in the quote.

Do I have to write everything?

You know the business; we shape it into page copy. Premium includes more writing help. You still approve anything that describes prices, hours, or medical-style services.

Can you add booking or a menu?

Yes. We design the menu on the page and link a booking button to the system you already use, such as a calendar or reservation tool. We do not invent a new booking product.

What does “from $299” mean?

It is the starting price for the listed scope. Extra pages, custom photography, or a complicated booking setup are quoted before work starts.

Do you host the site?

Premium includes three months of maintenance and hosting. Other packages can add hosting, or we can launch on a host you already pay for.

Will it work on phones?

Yes. Every layout is designed phone-first, then expanded for larger screens. That is how most local customers arrive.
