FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

How long does it actually take? +

The draft — homepage, about, and product pages — is generated in this browser in a few minutes. You need a name, a category, photos, and a price for each item. Payments and shipping start after you send the publish form.

Do I need professional photos? +

No. Phone photos are enough to start. On the published store we clean up the background and the lighting. You can replace any photo later.

Can I edit the store after it is published? +

Yes. Product names, prices, descriptions, photos, and page copy can be edited without a revision fee. That is part of the $29 month.

What is included in the price? +

$39 once, to set the store up, and $29 a month for hosting, SSL, and the editor. Card processing fees are charged by Stripe. Shipping rates come from USPS, UPS, and FedEx at checkout.

Who is this for? +

Independent businesses selling in the United States: local shops, Etsy-style sellers, side hustlers, food brands, and people who make things. The store is set up for Stripe, U.S. sales tax, and domestic shipping.
