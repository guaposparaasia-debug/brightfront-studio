FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

What does it cost? +

$250 upfront so we start building, $250 after the website is built and you approve it, then $39 a month for maintenance (hosting, updates and changes). The two $250 payments are invoiced by email after we contact you.

When do I pay what? +

First $250 to begin. Second $250 only after you've seen the finished website and approved it. The $39/month maintenance starts once the site is approved and live. We email the finished site and the payment links from info@brightfrontdesign.com.

What does the $39 a month cover? +

Hosting, keeping your site secure and up to date, and changes you request, like new hours, a new menu or fresh photos. It's month to month with no contract, and you can cancel anytime.

Do I need to supply photos or text? +

Phone photos are enough to start. Tell us what you do, send what you have, and we'll shape it into clear, good-looking pages. You can send more or replace anything later.

Will it work on phones? +

Yes. Every website we build is mobile-friendly and tested on small screens first, because that's where most local customers will find you.

Who is this for? +

Small, independent businesses such as cafes, salons, bakeries, food trucks and local stores, and anyone who wants a simple, professional website without doing the work themselves.
