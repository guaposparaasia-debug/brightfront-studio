The problem

Customers Google you first.

Before they cross the street, they search your name, your neighbourhood, or “near me.” A missing site, a broken phone page, or a menu that lives only in a photo can send them to the next result. The website is the door they meet before the real one.

They search the nameA clean page with hours, address text, and what you actually sell beats a blank result.
They judge in secondsType, photos, and a clear next step — call, book, or visit — do the talking.
They pick what looks openA current menu or booking link makes a Tuesday afternoon feel looked-after.

FAQ

Questions owners actually ask.

Is the preview really free?

Yes. The homepage preview is free. No card needed, and no obligation. If you don’t love it, you walk away and owe nothing.

How do I pay?

Only after you approve the preview. You pay securely via PayPal (cards accepted): $59 today covers setup and your first month, then $29 a month.

Do I pay anything up front?

Yes - $59 today covers setup and your first month. After that it's $29 a month. No contract; cancel anytime in your PayPal account.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime, with no contract or cancellation fee. Cancel in your PayPal account under automatic payments, or email info@brightfrontdesign.com and we’ll cancel it for you. No further charges are taken after you cancel. Your site and your domain stay yours either way.

Do I need a PayPal account?

PayPal treats the $29/month as an automatic payment. Depending on your country, PayPal may let you pay by debit or credit card without an account; otherwise you can create a free PayPal account at checkout.

How long does a site take?

A Starter page is often ready in about a week once we have your text and photos. Business and Premium take longer if menus, booking tools, or extra pages are involved. We confirm a date in the quote.

Do I have to write everything?

You know the business; we shape it into page copy. Premium includes more writing help. You still approve anything that describes prices, hours, or medical-style services.

Can you add booking or a menu?

Yes. We design the menu on the page and link a booking button to the system you already use, such as a calendar or reservation tool. We do not invent a new booking product.

What does it cost?

$59 setup, then $29 a month. Price is locked. Cancel anytime.

Do you host the site?

Yes. Hosting, SSL, and an uptime check are included in the $29 a month.

Can you get me a domain name?

Yes. Domain setup is $49 plus the domain cost itself (usually $10–15/year; we recommend prepaying 2–3 years). We register the domain, connect it to your site, and can transfer it to your own account anytime — it’s always yours. The exact domain cost is quoted by email before anything is charged. Already have a domain, or prefer to buy it yourself? We connect it for free.

Will it work on phones?

Yes. Every layout is designed phone-first, then expanded for larger screens. That is how most local customers arrive.

What kind of software can you build?

Small, practical tools: booking systems, order and inventory trackers, customer lists with reminders, automatic notifications, invoices and quotes, staff schedules, dashboards, and AI chat assistants. If it would save you time every week, ask us.

How much does custom software cost?

Every project gets a custom quote. Simple tools start from $499, and monthly care is available. The consultation and mini demo are free, so you see what you’re getting before you pay anything.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Describe the problem in plain words; we build it, set it up, and show you and your staff how to use it.
