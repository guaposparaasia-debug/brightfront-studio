Story

A family truck, painted like a folk-art parade.

Alebrijes is family-run. Luz Maria Garcia makes the salsas. Her sons Mike and Alex run the truck with her. The truck itself is painted with alebrijes, the colorful Oaxacan folk-art creatures the name comes from.

They park at 1500 E Long St in King-Lincoln Bronzeville, near the MLK Library. Weekday mornings and lunch only — closed Saturday and Sunday.

People come back for the smoky charcoal-grilled flavor, the salsas, the huge burritos, and a friendly crew at the window.
