Our story

A taste of home, from Hilo to Keizer.

Aloha All Day opened in Keizer in late 2024, and island folks found it fast. Customers raised in Hilo, Maui and Oahu say it’s the best plate lunch they’ve had on the mainland, with mac salad that tastes just like home.

The crew is friendly and high-energy, the portions feed the whole family, and the food often sells out, so come early. Regulars rave about the Auntie plate, the musubi and the ribs.
