About the shop

“Happiest I’ve been with a haircut in a long time.”

Back Road Barber is the La Grange shop where Taken, Jodi, Danielle, Tiffany and Natalie keep regulars coming back. Customers walk in on a lunch break and leave with a quick, well-done cut—and kids beg to return for the chess and games.

Tiffany has been called the best among fifty-plus barbershops; Natalie leaves people “feeling like a million bucks.” Danielle teaches kids how to style their hair. Cuts run about $20, and they’ll fix anything before you leave.
