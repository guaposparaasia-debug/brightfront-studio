Our bakery

Fresh every morning, the traditional way.

The Bakers Dozen is a traditional bakers on the corner of Splott Road, baking bread, cakes, pastries and hot food fresh each day for the local community — and for wholesale customers too.

Rolls are made fresh to order, the Welsh cakes are light and fluffy, and the kids love the dinosaur-shaped Cornish pasties. Get there early: regulars warn the best bits sell out. The shop is step-free and pram and wheelchair friendly.
