About the shop

“Best haircut I’ve had in years.”

Barbershop of Trenton is the North Miami Street shop with that old-school feel customers say you can’t find anywhere else. It’s clean, professional and family friendly—Jason and Jeff are great with teens and kids alike.

Regulars have trusted Jason for up to seven years: every style, done exactly right. Don’t miss the hot towel razor shave, and bring your beard. Park in the rear and come in through the back door. Book anytime on Booksy, with late hours Monday and Tuesday.
