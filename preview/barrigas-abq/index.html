The family

West Side tacos, made with hospitality.

Barrigas Abq is a family taco truck on Central Avenue SW. Regulars call it one of the best food trucks on the West Side, and first-timers keep mentioning the same thing: the welcome. The owner is patient, quick and happy to suggest his favorites.

The asada and pastor are seasoned to perfection, the birria tacos come with a rich consomé, and the salsas are, in one customer’s words, “top tier.” Add a dragon fruit–pineapple agua fresca and you’re set.
