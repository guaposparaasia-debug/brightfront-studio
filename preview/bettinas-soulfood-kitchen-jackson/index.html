The kitchen

“Though she may be small, she is mighty.”

Bettina's is a quaint little soul food diner in a classic downtown building on South Gallatin Street. The dining room has just a handful of tables, but the flavors and the portions are big.

Lunch works plate by plate: choose your main—fried or baked chicken, catfish, neckbones, red beans and rice or meatloaf—then add sides like candied yams, mac and cheese, greens, black-eyed peas or lima beans, with cornbread and dessert.
