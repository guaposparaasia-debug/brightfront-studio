Our bakery

“Cakes that make my dreams come true.”

Brandi's Bakery and Bistro is the Berea shop where custom cakes come out even better than the picture you brought in. Customers have trusted Brandi for graduations, birthdays and parties for years—and she walks every cake out and explains how to keep it perfect.

Stop in for lunch—an Italian chicken panini gets rave reviews—and finish with something sweet: pumpkin bundt with chocolate icing, cupcakes or chocolate-covered strawberries. The shop is always decorated festively, calm and relaxing.
