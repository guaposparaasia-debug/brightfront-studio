Our shop

Old-fashioned baking, tucked away in Eltham.

Brent’s is a family-run patisserie a few steps off Eltham’s main strip — “quiet, calm and personable,” as one local put it. Inside: fresh cakes, pastries, pies and coffee, and a team (Stuart included) who make ordering a celebration cake easy.

The passionfruit sponge is the most popular thing in the cabinet: old-fashioned, light as a feather, filled with fresh cream and topped with passionfruit icing. Keep an eye on Facebook for the odd two-for-one vanilla slice morning.
