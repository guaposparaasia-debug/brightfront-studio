About the truck

North-east Adelaide’s late-night street food fix.

Brothers Food Corner serves Punjabi and Indo-Chinese street food from a truck on North East Road, open into the night when most kitchens have closed. One guest stumbled on it at 9:30pm and called it their new go-to spot after Mehak and Harman went above and beyond.

Expect the flavours of an Indian street stall: pani puri that’s fresh and tangy, buttery chaap, and Indo-Chinese favourites like chilli chicken and noodles — plus the creative noodles burger that has become the truck’s signature.
