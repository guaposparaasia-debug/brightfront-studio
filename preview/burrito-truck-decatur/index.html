The truck

A Pershing Road staple.

Burrito Truck has been a staple on East Pershing Road for years. Owner Raymundo Padron has run the truck at this spot for five years, serving Decatur day in and day out.

Regulars say it’s the best street tacos in town: meats perfectly seasoned, food always hot off the grill, and fast, friendly service. Some come twice a week. Bring cash, grab a seat at the picnic tables, and come before the truck closes for the season.
