Meet Maryam

Life is short. Eat the fancy cake.

Caketopia is Maryam’s patisserie in Canning Vale, focused on fresh, homemade cakes, cookies, pastries and Persian delights for birthdays, parties and corporate events.

Clients say she really listens and delivers something better than they imagined — from a Cinderella cake and a KPop Demon Hunters cake for little ones to elegant 18th and 21st birthday cakes. She’ll even suggest a lighter buttercream so the cake isn’t too rich.
