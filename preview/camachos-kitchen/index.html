The truck

A taste of Honduras on Alexandria Drive.

Camacho's Kitchen brings real Honduran home cooking to Lexington. One guest wrote: “You won’t believe what Camacho is able to whip up in his small truck!” Another, who moved here from New Orleans, says it rates right up with the Honduran food back there.

Everything is cooked to order, so some dishes take a little longer — a whole fried fish is worth the wait. Portions are big enough that one order lasted a guest three meals.

  • Cooked fresh to order
  • Big portions
