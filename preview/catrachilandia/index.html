Nuestra historia · Our story

From an apartment kitchen to a truck on Millbrook.

Catrachilandia is run by four sisters from Comayagua, Honduras. They started selling from their apartment. Now they have a food truck in Raleigh — and a dream of opening a restaurant.

Customers come for the big, filling plates, the authentic flavor, and the kind sisters who help you choose. There is a covered seating area beside the truck.
