About us

Low prices, high dedication.

Darra Dog Grooming is a family-owned, home-based salon. Grooming is their passion, and they aim to make every visit a positive experience — keeping prices low and dedication high.

Shelley and Donna are known for patience with scaredy-cats (like Remus, “scared of his own shadow”) and for squeezing dogs in on the day when another groomer cancels. Moodles, border collies and puggos all go home fresh and fluffy.
