The salon

Greeted by name, every visit.

One longtime client remembers her very first visit: Tammy greeted every person who walked in by name. Some guests have been coming to Tammy for more than 16 years, and her daughter Trang brings any design you can describe to life.

The salon keeps real appointments instead of making you wait behind walk-ins, keeps its tools and counters spotless, and only uses the drill when it’s needed. Now there’s a coffee and boba bar inside, so your pedicure comes with a drink.
