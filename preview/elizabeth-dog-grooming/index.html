Meet Elizabeth

Calm hands, clean cuts, happy dogs.

Owners keep using the same words about Elizabeth: patient, sweet, professional. She takes her time with first-time and anxious dogs so the visit feels stress-free, and she is clearly experienced with big breeds — standard poodles, Goldendoodles and Irish Terriers included.

Some families have been coming since their dog was a puppy. Others found her this year and say they’re “stuck with her” now. Prices are affordable, and she is quick to reply when you message.
