A small-town diner, kept the old way.

Fina's is a family-owned diner with a small, cozy room, local team photos on the walls and Coca-Cola memorabilia on every shelf. Maria is usually the one who greets you at the door.

The menu runs New Mexican and American: chile red or green, eggs any way, burgers at lunch. Travelers make it their breakfast stop for every morning of a trip.
