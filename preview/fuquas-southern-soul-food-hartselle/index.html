The family table

“The atmosphere is like a family get together.”

Fuqua's Southern Soul Food is the kind of place regulars call home. Mrs. Kris and Mr. Mo greet everybody like family, good music plays while you eat, and the dining room is full of folks bantering with the staff.

The buffet is old-school Southern cooking: crispy fried chicken, fried pork chops, pulled pork with real bark, mashed potatoes, greens and cornbread, a fresh salad bar, and homemade desserts included with the buffet.
