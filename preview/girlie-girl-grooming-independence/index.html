About us

“The spa” your dog actually loves.

Girlie Girl Grooming is a friendly dog grooming salon on East 23rd Street in Independence. Clients say the ladies treat their dogs with the same care their own families do, and take their time to get every groom right.

Nervous pups are welcome: a puppy-mill rescue poodle gets to hang out in the lobby instead of a crate, and express service makes first visits easy. Clients say the pricing is better than anywhere else their dogs have been groomed.
