The family

“Best food & service in town & the owners are great people.”

Goodness Gracious is family owned and operated: Daniel runs the front while his mom and sister do the cooking. Classic jazz piano plays, the owners stop by your table, and travelers passing through Tupelo say they felt the warmth and love the moment they walked in.

This isn’t fast food—it’s real home cooking. Smothered pork chops, meatloaf, BBQ chicken, chicken spaghetti, mac and cheese, mashed potatoes and gravy, greens, fried corn and cornbread, plus daily specials and a 2-meat combo.
