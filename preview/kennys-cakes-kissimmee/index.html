Our bakery

Your idea, baked into a cake.

Kenny’s Cakes is a Puerto Rican bakery on West Dakin Avenue in downtown Kissimmee. Bring a picture or just an idea, and the team will help you pick the flavor, colors and design. Customers say the finished cake goes “beyond anything I imagined.”

Guests talk about cakes that are thick, moist and beautiful, and about kind, accommodating service, even for a last-minute order. Families have also celebrated quinceañeras in the activities room, with packages that cover decorations, food and cake.
