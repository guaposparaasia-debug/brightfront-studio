The truck

Finally, a Puerto Rican spot in the area. ¡Wepa!

La Parada del Coquí brings real Puerto Rican home cooking to the New River Valley. Truckers say it’s their new spot for a home-cooked meal, and locals are already regulars.

Everything is fresh and made to order, and the team shows you your food before bagging it so there are no surprises when you open the box. One guest summed it up: “feels like a warm hug!”
