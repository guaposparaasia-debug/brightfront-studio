Meet Steven & Reia

“Hi, friend!” Cake made with patience.

Layers is the Vineville Avenue bakery run by Steven and Reia Collins for eight years. Walk in and you’ll be greeted with a hearty “hi, friend!”, and there’s a good chance Steven will hand you a sample before you choose.

Their cakes come by the slice or whole, and Macon keeps rewarding them: Layers won the Cherry Blossom Festival’s Pink Provisions fan vote, then its best Sweet Treat award. Customers say it feels like visiting friends, and that it’s worth the stop off I-75.
