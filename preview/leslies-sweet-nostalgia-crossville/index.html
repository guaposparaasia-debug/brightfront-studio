A kitchen with nothing to worry about

Everything here is free of gluten, dairy and oats, so you can choose from the whole case without asking twice.

Leslie is a pastry chef by trade. She bakes with fruit and eggs from local farms, so the case changes with the season, and she is happy to explain every ingredient.

Walk in only. Leslie does not take advance or phone orders.
