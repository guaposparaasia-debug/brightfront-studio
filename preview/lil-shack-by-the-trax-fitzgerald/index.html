The Shack

“THE BEST BBQ in town, NO DOUBT.”

Lil Shack by the Trax is the cozy little spot on North Grant Street in Fitzgerald where everything is made from scratch—down to the bacon ranch dressing and the lemonade.

Regulars call ahead to ask when the ribs come out of the smoker. Others swear by the brisket sandwich with tots, the reuben, the chicken-salad trio and the carrot cake. There’s a weekly special, healthy choices next to the comfort food, and catering for your next event.
