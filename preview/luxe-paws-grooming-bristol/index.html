Meet Felicia

“They treat your pup like their own.”

Families have followed Felicia for years—through two poodles, a golden doodle, an Australian Shepherd and a 15-year-old Shih Tzu mix—and now to her new salon on Wood Street in Bristol.

Owners say she’s very patient with nervous dogs, great with large breeds, and that the shop is always clean. Some pups come every two weeks just for their nails and always come home happy.
