About the shop

“The best barber in town.”

Main Street Barber Shop is the upbeat downtown Shelbyville shop where Tommy and Emily treat regulars like family. Customers come out “feeling like a new man,” catch up on what’s going on in the community, and bring their whole family back for years.

Parents say Tommy is beyond patient: one mom shared that her son with autism felt comfortable and never had a meltdown. Booking through Square is easy, same-day spots often open up, and Tommy is respectful of your time—so book ahead.
