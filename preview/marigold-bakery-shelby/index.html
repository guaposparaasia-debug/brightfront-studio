Our bakery

A true gem of Shelby.

Marigold Bakery is the downtown Shelby spot run by sisters and friends who serve up “baked greats.” Locals drive over from Mansfield for Boston cream donuts and the apple chai scone that steals the show—strong chai, apple chunks, rich and fluffy.

There’s pour-over coffee, mini pumpkin bundts, cookies and iced holiday treats, plus gluten-free options. Families love the quiet atmosphere: grab a treat, then read on the couch. Shelby Daily Globe has covered the grand opening and spotlighted the business.
