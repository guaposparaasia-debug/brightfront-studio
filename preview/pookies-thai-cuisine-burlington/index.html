Simple, clean and close

A long, narrow dining room in downtown Burlington, run by a small family. The menu is short and cooked fresh each day, the portions are big, and the young staff are friendly and attentive.

Want it vegetarian? Ask for the tofu stir-fry, even though it is not on the board.
