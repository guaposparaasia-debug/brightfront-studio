The shop

From a living-room chair to the Coast’s favorite shop.

Owner Kyla cut hair in her living room for well over a year while carefully planning this shop. Popps opened in 2025 and just celebrated its first year — even with road construction out front, the crew stood outside waving at slow-moving traffic.

Kyla, Zana and Mr. Shump are known for precise fades, patient kids’ cuts (double crowns included) and a proper old-school touch: a hot towel and razor shave on the neck or beard with every service.

  • Walk-ins only
  • Hot towel with every cut
  • Straight-razor shaves
  • Kids welcome
