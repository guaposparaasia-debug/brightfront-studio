About the shop

“The best place for a guy to get a haircut and neck shave!”

Rhodes Barber Shop has been taking care of Anderson for years—men, women, children, anybody. Customers say the team often meets them at the door, and you can always hear laughter and good conversation inside.

Barbers like McKenzie and Hannah have loyal regulars who drive 45 minutes to see them. There are refreshments, quick turnaround and fair prices, and the stylists do beautiful color work, too.
