The family

“Some of the best street tacos I’ve had in my life.”

Riquisimo is the father-son taco truck on Lewis and Clark Parkway that southern Indiana keeps talking about. They know regulars by name, hand out samples of the cochinita pibil, and explain how it’s made back in Mexico.

Customers swear by the quesabirrias with consomé, the adobada with that green sauce, and the asada tacos. Save room: mom makes the tres leches cakes, and the last slice goes fast. Open Thursday through Sunday evenings, late on weekends.
