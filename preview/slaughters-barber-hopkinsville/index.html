About the shop

Family-owned, wonderful people.

Slaughter’s is a family-owned barbershop on Lafayette Road in Hopkinsville—father and daughter, same barbers you can count on. Customers say it has the old-school feel with modern amenities: clean, friendly, fair prices and a solid cut every time.

Barber Jeff is known for close attention to detail and patience with little ones learning to trust haircuts. Moms say it’s their teenage boys’ favorite barber—he does more than others without the exorbitant cost. Walk-ins only; first come, first served.
