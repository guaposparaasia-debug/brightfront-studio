The cart

A bit of Sonora, on South 12th.

Owner Pablo grew up in different towns across Sonora, Mexico. He dreamed of owning a hot dog stand since he was a kid. Now he serves a bit of that Sonoran childhood from the cart in Tucson.

There is a covered seating area. Cash and cards. Catering for events and parties.
