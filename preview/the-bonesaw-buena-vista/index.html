Meet the crew

Comfort food, made by friends.

The BoneSaw is a Buena Vista food trailer that locals call a hidden gem. Regulars say it feels like visiting friends: the owners are kind, generous and fun to talk to, and the menu is creative while still approachable.

Call your order in and it’s ready, hot and crazy quick, which makes it the easiest lunch in town. Seth and Jewel also cater: one family raved about their BBQ for about 80 guests, and another couple asked them to cater their wedding.
