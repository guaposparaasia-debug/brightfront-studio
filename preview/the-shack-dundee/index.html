Gill’s story

“I’d never made a burger until the night before I opened.”

Gill Young opened The Shack on Isla Street in 2016 — and, as she told The Courier, she’d never made a burger until the night before. Since then “Auntie Gill’s” burgers have become the stuff of Dundee legend, with folk travelling from as far as Montrose.

The Shack is famous for its banter as much as its food: dance music on, Gill calling out names mid-service, and a welcome like an old friend. In 2024 she added a second truck, “the baby Shack”, for weddings, corporate events and festivals.
