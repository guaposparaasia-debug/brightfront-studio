Our story

Coffee, food, art and music on East Colfax.

VIVA! is run by husband and wife Elisa Garcia and Leonardo Munoz-Corona. Elisa grew up in Denver with roots in Uruguay and runs the coffee; Leo, from Mexico, is the chef and bakes the pastries. They met in Singapore in 2013 and opened VIVA! in August 2024.

Elisa painted the murals herself, the old office is now a local art gallery, and the bookshelf folds down into a stage for music nights. Guests call it “just the kind of place East Colfax needs.”
