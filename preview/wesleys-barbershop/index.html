About Wes

A cornerstone of downtown Elberta.

Wes served his country honorably, and now he runs one of the best-loved barbershops in Baldwin County. Customers drive 20 miles — some even from Georgia — because he’s friendly, efficient and takes real pride in every cut.

It’s small but cozy and spotless, right next to the park, so plenty of families pair a haircut with playtime. Wes is great with kids, too: one dad says his one-year-old’s first haircut couldn’t have gone better.
