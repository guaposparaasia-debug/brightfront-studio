Privacy

How we handle your information

Brightfront Studio runs brightfrontdesign.com. This page explains, in plain terms, what the site collects today, why, and who else is involved.

Who we are

Brightfront Studio — brightfrontdesign.com. You can reach us at info@brightfrontdesign.com.

The publish form

When you publish a store from the builder on our homepage, the form asks for your name, email, store name, category, and the product names, prices, and descriptions you typed. We also note how many photos you selected.

The photos themselves stay in your browser. They are read locally to show the preview and are not uploaded when you publish.

On submit, the details above are sent to our server (a Cloudflare Pages Function at /api/builder-lead), stored so we can follow up, and passed to an internal alert so our team sees the request. Your browser also opens a pre-filled email draft to info@brightfrontdesign.com, which you can choose to send or not. Along with the request we record the page it came from, your browser's user agent, and your IP address, which we use to limit abuse.

Payments

  • PayPal. The subscription button takes you to PayPal's own checkout. PayPal may send us payment notifications that include your name, email, and transaction IDs. We use these to send a thank-you email and to help with support.
  • Lemon Squeezy. The card checkout is an embedded Lemon Squeezy form. Lemon Squeezy handles your card details; we do not see or store them.

The /thanks/ page is only a confirmation screen after payment. Our homepage may mention Stripe as a payment option for the stores we build for you; checkout on this site itself is PayPal and Lemon Squeezy.

Browser storage and chat

The site may save a small note in your browser's local storage (key bf_builder_lead) to remember a referral or lead hint and prefill the form for you. It stays on your device.

An AI chat assistant is not currently shown on the homepage. The files for it (/chat-widget.js and /api/chat) exist on this site and may be turned on later. If it is enabled and you use it, your messages and a session ID are processed to answer you, and an email address you type into the chat may be saved as a lead.

Hosting and third parties

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which may process your IP address, user agent, and standard request logs to deliver and protect the site.

Pages load a few outside resources: Google Fonts for typography, the Tailwind CSS CDN for styling, and Lemon Squeezy's script for checkout. These providers receive standard request information when your browser fetches their files. We do not use Google Analytics on the homepage.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

This site uses Google AdSense (publisher ID ca-pub-1521418120439711) to show ads. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.

Learn more about how Google uses data for advertising in Google’s advertising technologies policies. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Your choices

  • You can browse the site without filling in the form or paying.
  • You can remove anything stored in your browser by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.
  • To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email info@brightfrontdesign.com.

If how we handle information changes, we will update this page.
