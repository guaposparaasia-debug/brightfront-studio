Getting started

How to get a website without doing it yourself

You don't need a designer's eye, a builder account or professional photos. You need a few minutes to tell us about your shop and whatever photos you have on your phone.

Updated November 2026 · Brightfront Studio

What to send us

  • Your email, so we can send you the finished website.
  • A short description. What you sell or offer, where you are, and what the site should include.
  • A few photos. Phone photos are enough to start: your storefront, your work, your food or products.
  • Helpful extras, if you have them: your logo, your hours, your menu or price list, and links to your Instagram, Facebook or booking app.

No logo? No problem. Missing something? Send what you have and add the rest later.

How it works, step by step

  1. Tell us what you want. Use the request form on our homepage.
  2. Pay $250 to start. The first payment is due right after you send your request, so we can begin building.
  3. We build your website. Your photos, hours, menu or services, laid out cleanly for phones first.
  4. You review and approve. We email you the finished site from info@brightfrontdesign.com. If something isn't right, tell us before you approve.
  5. Pay the remaining $250 after you approve. You only pay the second half once you've seen the finished website and you're happy with it.
  6. $39/month keeps it running. Hosting, updates and changes, month to month. Email us whenever something changes.

What you don't have to do

  • Choose a template or learn a website builder.
  • Write all the text yourself. We shape your notes into clear pages.
  • Set up or manage hosting.
  • Remember a login to change your hours later.

Start with a short request

Leave your email, describe the website you want and add a few photos. Then pay the first $250 so we can start building.
