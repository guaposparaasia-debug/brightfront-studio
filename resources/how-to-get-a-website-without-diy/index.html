Getting started

How to get a website without doing it yourself

You do not need a designer's eye, a builder account or professional photos. You need a few minutes to tell us about your shop and whatever photos you have on your phone. Here is the whole process, step by step.

Who this is for

Most small shop owners do not want a website project. They want a website, and they want it to be right. If you have tried a builder and given up halfway, or never started because the template choices felt overwhelming, this way of working is for you. You bring the knowledge of your business; we bring the layout, the writing and the technical side.

What to send us

  • Your email, so we can send you the finished website.
  • A short description. What you sell or offer, where you are, and what the site should include.
  • A few photos. Phone photos are enough to start: your storefront, your work, your food or products.
  • Helpful extras, if you have them: your logo, your hours, your menu or price list, and links to your Instagram, Facebook or booking app.

No logo? No problem. Missing something? Send what you have and add the rest later. If you want a complete list, use our preparation checklist.

A sample request

Your description does not need to be long. Something like this is plenty to start:

"Corner cafe in the old town, open since 2019. Breakfast and lunch, good coffee, homemade cakes. Open Tue to Sun, 8 to 4. I'd like people to see our menu, photos of the food and the room, and be able to call or get directions. We're on Instagram."

How it works, step by step

  1. Tell us what you want. Use the request form on our homepage: your email, a description and up to five photos.
  2. Pay $250 to start. The first payment is due right after you send your request, so we can begin building.
  3. We build your website. Your photos, hours, menu or services, laid out cleanly for phones first. Once we have your photos and details, we aim to send the first version within about 3 business days.
  4. You review it. We email you the site from info@brightfrontdesign.com. If something is not right, tell us before you approve.
  5. Pay the remaining $250 after you approve. You only pay the second half once you have seen the finished website and you are happy with it.
  6. $49/month keeps it running. Hosting, updates and changes, month to month. Email us whenever something changes.

How to review the first version

Reviewing is the one part where your attention really matters. Take ten minutes and go through it like a customer would:

  • Open it on your phone first, since that is where most visitors will see it
  • Check every fact: name, address, phone number, hours, prices
  • Tap the call button and the directions link to make sure they work
  • Read the text out loud; does it sound like your business?
  • Look at the photos; is anything you are proud of missing?
  • Show it to a regular customer or a friend and ask what they would change

Then reply with everything in one email. Clear, specific notes like "use the photo of the front window at the top" or "we close at 3 on Sundays" are easiest to act on.

What happens to your photos

We look through everything you send and choose the strongest images for each part of the page: usually one wide, welcoming photo at the top, your best products or work further down, and the outside of the shop near the address so people recognize it. We crop and resize them so the page loads quickly on mobile data. If a photo you love does not appear, just ask; it may have been too dark or blurry, and a quick retake often fixes that.

What you don't have to do

  • Choose a template or learn a website builder.
  • Write all the text yourself. We shape your notes into clear pages.
  • Set up or manage hosting.
  • Remember a login to change your hours later.

After launch

Once the site is live, put the link everywhere people already find you: your Instagram bio, your Google Business Profile, your email signature, even a small sign or card at the counter. When anything changes, email us. That is what the $49/month Care Plan is for.

Common questions

What if I only have a few photos?

Send what you have. Three or four good phone photos are enough to start, and you can send more any time.

Do I need to write anything?

Only a few sentences of notes in your own words. We turn them into clear text for the site.

What if I want changes after I approve?

Once the site is live, changes are covered by the $49/month Care Plan. Email us what you would like changed.

How do I pay?

Through PayPal; cards are accepted at PayPal checkout. The first $250 is paid on our homepage after you send your request, and we send the request for the second $250 with your finished site.
