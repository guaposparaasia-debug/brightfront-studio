One page or several pages for a local shop?

Most small shops are well served by one clear page with everything a customer needs. Separate pages start to help when you have a lot to say about different things.

Updated November 2026 · Brightfront Studio

Why one page often works best

  • It's quick on a phone. Visitors scroll once and see your photos, hours, menu or services, and how to reach you.
  • Nothing gets lost. There are no menus to dig through and no forgotten page with old information.
  • It's easy to keep accurate. One place to update when your hours or prices change.

A good one-pager for a cafe, salon or food truck usually has a welcome and a photo, what you offer, prices or a menu, hours and location, and a clear way to call, book or order.

When separate pages make sense

  • You have a long menu or many services that deserve their own page.
  • You serve clearly different groups, for example walk-in customers and catering or events.
  • You have several locations, each with its own hours and address.
  • You want to publish news, events or seasonal offers regularly.

A simple way to decide

Write down what a first-time customer needs to know before they visit. If it fits comfortably on one scrolling page, start with one page. You can add more later as the business grows. Tell us what you need in your request and we'll suggest the simplest layout that works.
