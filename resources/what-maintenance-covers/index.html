Maintenance

What the $39/month maintenance covers

After your website is approved and live, $39 a month keeps it online, secure and up to date, and covers the changes you ask for. There's no contract. You can cancel anytime.

Updated November 2026 · Brightfront Studio

What's included

  • Hosting and SSL. Your website is hosted for you and served over HTTPS, the padlock in the browser. No servers or hosting accounts for you to manage.
  • Keeping it current. We keep the site working and up to date.
  • Updates to your information. Hours, holiday closures, menus, prices, photos and text.
  • Changes on request. Email us what you'd like different and we make the change.
  • Mobile-friendly, always. Changes are made to look right on phones, tablets and desktops.
  • A person to talk to. Email info@brightfrontdesign.com and someone from our team answers.

Examples of changes people send us

  • "We're closed Monday for the holiday."
  • "New fall menu attached, please swap it in."
  • "Prices for cuts are going up next month."
  • "Here are three new photos of the shop."
  • "We started taking bookings online, here's the link."

When it starts and how to stop

You pay $250 to start the build and $250 after you approve the finished website. The $39/month starts once your site is approved and live. It's month to month, so you can cancel anytime with no contract.

Planning something much bigger than an update, like a full redesign? Email us first and we'll tell you up front what's involved.
