About these tools

We build websites for small shops and new businesses, and the same three questions come up again and again: how much do I need to start, what should I charge, and what is my plan? These tools help you answer them in a few minutes.

They run entirely in your browser. There's no sign-up, and nothing you type is sent to us. The results are rough planning estimates, not financial, tax or legal advice; rules and costs differ by country and state.
