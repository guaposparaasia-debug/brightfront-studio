How the calculator works

It splits your costs into two kinds. One-time costs (equipment, licenses, first stock and other set-up costs) are paid once, before or as you open. Monthly costs (rent and marketing) keep coming every month.

The startup total adds every one-time cost to your first month of rent and marketing. The runway reserve multiplies your monthly costs by the months of runway you choose, so you can keep paying the bills while sales build up. The big figure at the bottom is the two added together: the cash to have ready before you open.

What to do with the result

  • Check each line twice. Get a real quote for anything over a few hundred dollars instead of guessing.
  • Add a buffer. Something always costs more than planned. Many owners add 10–20% on top of the total.
  • Look for lines you can shrink. Second-hand equipment, a smaller first stock order or working from home for the first months can cut the total a lot.
  • Put the number in your plan. Your startup budget is one of the nine parts of a one-page business plan.

If you are not sure what belongs on the list, read our guide to how much it costs to start a small business.

Common questions

Is the first month of rent counted twice?

No. The startup total includes one month of rent and marketing, because you pay them before or as you open. The runway reserve covers the months after that, so the two never overlap.

What should I put under licenses and permits?

Anything you must pay once before you can trade: business registration, local permits, health or trade inspections, and similar fees. Rules and costs differ by city, state and country, so check with your local council or a qualified adviser.

How many months of runway should I plan for?

Many new owners plan for three to six months of running costs, because sales usually build slowly. If your business needs a long sales cycle, or you are leaving a job to do it, a longer runway is safer.

Is my data saved or sent anywhere?

No. The calculator runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to us or stored on a server.
